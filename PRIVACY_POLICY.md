## Information we collect

**Account information:** When you sign up, we collect your name, phone
number or email address, and the location details you provide (state,
district, mandal, village).

**Location:** If you choose to tap "Use my current location" when posting
a land, reporting a disease alert, or as an admin adding a vet, we
collect your device's GPS coordinates at that moment. This is optional —
the app works without it, just without distance-based sorting for that
listing.

**Photos:** If you upload a photo for a land or vet listing, that photo
is stored and shown to other users of the app.

**Content you post:** Land listings, booking requests, disease alert
reports, and any text you type into the app are stored and may be shown
to other users as part of the app's normal function (e.g., other farmers
can see a land listing you post).

## How we use this information
Solely to operate the app's features: showing your listings to other
users, calculating distance/directions, processing booking requests, and
displaying disease alerts to the community. We do not sell your
information to third parties.

## Where data is stored
Data is stored using Google Firebase (Firestore database and
Authentication), a third-party cloud service. Firebase's own privacy
practices apply to how they process data on our behalf:
https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

## Data sharing
Information you post (land listings, vet contact numbers, disease
alerts) is visible to other signed-in users of the app, as that's the
core purpose of a farmer-to-farmer marketplace and community alert
system. We do not share your data with advertisers.
