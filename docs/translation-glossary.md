sees one term for one idea everywhere. Wording is plain, village-level
Telugu and Hindi (no Sanskritised or Urdu-heavy forms where a common word exists).

## Review method used
1. Write each string from the meaning, not word by word.
2. Back-translate to English and compare with the source.
3. Check the glossary term is used, numbers and units are unchanged, and the
   sentence reads naturally aloud to a farmer.
4. `src/i18n/translations.test.js` checks every key exists in all three languages.
