## Full description (max 4000 characters)

JeevaMitra connects livestock owners and land providers in one simple app —
built for real farmers, in their own language.

🌾 FIND & BOOK GRAZING LAND
Browse available grazing and fodder land near you, see real distance and
directions, and request a booking directly from the app. Land owners
review and approve requests — no double-bookings, ever.

🐄 POST YOUR OWN LAND
Have grazing or fodder land to offer? List it in minutes — village,
district, acres, price, and a photo — and start receiving booking
requests from nearby livestock owners.

🩺 FIND A VERIFIED VET
Browse a curated directory of veterinary officers and assistants —
government or private — filtered by service (vaccination, first aid,
artificial insemination, and more), with one-tap call, WhatsApp, and
directions.

📢 DISEASE ALERTS
See and report livestock disease outbreaks near you — species, severity,
symptoms — so your community can react quickly and protect their animals.

🌐 YOUR LANGUAGE
Use the app in English, Telugu (తెలుగు), or Hindi (हिन्दी) — switch
anytime with one tap.

📍 REAL LOCATION, NO GUESSWORK
Every land, vet, and alert can be tagged with real GPS location, so you
always know how far away something is — with one-tap turn-by-turn
directions in Google Maps.

Built for the way farmers actually work: simple, direct, and in the
language you're comfortable with.
